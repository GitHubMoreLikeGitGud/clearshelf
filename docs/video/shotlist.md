# ClearShelf demo video: shot list (round-3 re-cut)

File `clearshelf-demo-v2.mp4` (uploaded separately, not committed), re-cut on 25 Oct 2026 from the same 23 Oct footage: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 3:13.9 (193.9 s), H.264 + AAC, loudness normalised to -14 LUFS. Captions are burned in and also provided as `clearshelf-demo.srt`.

All screen footage is a headless-browser capture of the local stack on one PC on 23 October 2026: the ClearShelf UI and agent server, the Zetaris gateway, Zetaris Lightning in Docker and Nemotron 3 Nano 4B in Ollama. Nothing was staged in the UI. One edit was made in post: on the Dark Chocolate label shot, the Open Food Facts ingredient text is cut down to a short quote (the removed words are blanked and marked with an ellipsis), and the label bar says so. The officer's approval reason was typed for the demo, and the label bar says so too. Speed changes (speed-ups and one slowed static shot) and the two frames held still are labelled on screen. Amber outlines were added in post on four shots to point at the text the narration cites; those shots say "outlines added". The cold open's second shot is the top of the same Plant-Based Puffs card, taken from 4 s earlier in the same take (before the page scrolled to its source cards). The recall-watch beat is a replay as of 10 October 2026 and carries the UI's own replay banner. The approval and the conflict resolution were made on camera by the local demo officer identity. Zetaris shots come from the Zetaris UI of the same instance; the login happened before capture and is not shown. A drawn pointer and click ripple mark where each scripted click lands; nothing else was added to the pages.

Data on screen: Open Food Facts (© Open Food Facts contributors, ODbL 1.0), openFDA (public domain, CC0; not endorsed by the FDA) and UK FSA allergy alerts (Open Government Licence v3.0), all read through Zetaris.

Still shots: the repository's architecture diagram `docs/architecture.png`, an evaluation card (numbers copied from `docs/eval-report.md`, `docs/eval/` and the README, all measured on 23 and 25 Oct 2026) and an end card with the title, track, team and attributions.

Narration: a synthetic text-to-speech voice. No face, no human voice, no music.
